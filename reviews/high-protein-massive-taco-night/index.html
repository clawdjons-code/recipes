High-Protein Massive Taco Night

A huge, high-protein taco-night setup built from lean chicken and steak, air-fried peppers and onions, cauliflower rice, a spoonful-per-taco creamy bean-salsa spread, and egg wraps. Full bean spread batch, for context

The whole dip batch estimates around 690 calories, 43g protein, and 927g. Most of it is leftover if you use one spoonful per taco.

Step timing

Active time49 min
Elapsed from scratch50–65 min
With prepped meat25–35 min
Step 1: Start the vegetables and cauliflower rice — active 12 min, elapsed 18–25 min

Slice the bell peppers and onions. Add them to the air fryer, season with garlic salt, garlic powder, and black pepper, and air fry at 390–400°F until softened, lightly charred, and slightly crisp, tossing every 5–7 minutes. Microwave the frozen cauliflower rice while the peppers and onions cook.

Step 2: Grill the chicken and steak — active 12 min, elapsed 38–45 min

Grill the cooked-weight target amount of chicken breast first on low heat until it reaches 165°F, about 15 minutes per side depending on thickness. Let it rest, then grill the lean steak about 4 minutes per side for medium rare, or to preference. Rest briefly, then cut both meats into bite-size pieces.

Step 3: Make the creamy bean-salsa spread — active 7 min, elapsed 7–9 min

In a microwave-safe bowl, combine the refried beans, Laughing Cow Light wedges, Velveeta shreds, garlic salt, garlic powder, and black pepper. Microwave about 3 minutes, then stir in the low-calorie sour cream, sriracha, and the full jar of salsa until smooth and creamy.

Step 4: Combine and drain the vegetable base — active 6 min, elapsed 6–8 min

Combine the air-fried peppers and onions with the microwaved cauliflower rice in a large container. Drain excess liquid, season lightly again if needed, and mix thoroughly.

Step 5: Warm the wraps — active 2 min, elapsed 2–3 min

Warm the egg wraps by setting them over the warm vegetable container lid, or microwave them under a damp paper towel for 15–30 seconds until flexible.

Step 6: Assemble the tacos — active 10 min, elapsed 10–15 min

Set out the vegetable base, chopped meats, bean-salsa spread, wraps, and a plate. For each taco, spread about one rounded spoonful of bean dip down the center, add vegetables, add meat, roll, and eat. Repeat until happily stuffed. Refrigerate leftover bean spread.
