Step timing

Step 1: Start vegetables and cauliflower rice - active 10 min, elapsed 18-25 min

Slice 250 g bell pepper and 200 g onion. Air fry at 390-400°F with garlic salt, garlic powder, and black pepper until softened and lightly charred. Microwave 250 g cauliflower medley or cauliflower rice while the peppers and onions cook.

Step 2: Cook the selected protein - active 8 min, elapsed 8-15 min

Cook the 93% ground beef until browned and cooked through. Portion about 170 g across the 6 tacos.

Step 3: Measure the bean dip spread - active 3 min, elapsed 3-5 min

Use the measured bean dip amount for the meal, logged here as 7 servings / 122 calories total. Save the rest if you made a larger batch.

Step 4: Combine and drain the vegetable base - active 5 min, elapsed 5-8 min

Combine the peppers, onions, and cauliflower. Drain excess liquid, season lightly again if needed, and mix thoroughly.

Step 5: Warm the wraps - active 2 min, elapsed 2-3 min

Warm the 6 Egglife wraps over the warm container lid or microwave under a damp paper towel for 15-30 seconds.

Step 6: Assemble the tacos - active 10 min, elapsed 10-15 min

Spread bean dip down each wrap, add the vegetable base, add the selected protein, roll, and eat. Repeat for all 6 tacos.

Assumptions

  • The MFP/logged base without meat is 480 calories and about 26.5 g protein, anchored from the prior MFP total of 70.2 g protein minus the 175 g beef protein. Calories are estimates with a 2% margin of error.
  • The corrected ~750-calorie reference is treated as 400 g of 93% ground beef at 750 calories; chicken and sirloin use their 400 g cooked reference estimates, then all proteins are rounded to nice default quantities near 320 meat calories.
  • Resulting totals: 93% ground beef: 170 g, about 799 cal total (783-815 with 2% margin), about 69 g total protein; Chicken breast: 200 g, about 810 cal total (794-826 with 2% margin), about 88 g total protein; Lean sirloin: 150 g, about 795 cal total (779-811 with 2% margin), about 68 g total protein.
  • Chicken food-safety boundary: cook to 165°F. Sirloin doneness is preference; rest before slicing. Ground beef should be cooked through.
  • Allergens/diet flags: contains egg wraps and beans/legumes; dairy depends on the bean dip formula used.
