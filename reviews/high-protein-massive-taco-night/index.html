A huge, high-protein taco-night setup built from lean chicken and steak, air-fried peppers and onions, cauliflower rice, creamy bean-salsa spread, and egg wraps. It is designed for maximum volume, comfort-food flavor, and fat-loss-friendly calories.

Step 1: Start the vegetables and cauliflower rice — active 12 min, elapsed 18–25 min

Slice the bell peppers and onions. Add them to the air fryer, season with garlic salt, garlic powder, and black pepper, and air fry at 390–400°F until softened, lightly charred, and slightly crisp, tossing every 5–7 minutes. Microwave the frozen cauliflower rice while the peppers and onions cook.

Step 2: Grill the chicken and steak — active 12 min, elapsed 38–45 min

Grill the cooked-weight target amount of chicken breast first on low heat until it reaches 165°F, about 15 minutes per side depending on thickness. Let it rest, then grill the lean steak about 4 minutes per side for medium rare, or to preference. Rest briefly, then cut both meats into bite-size pieces.

Step 3: Make the creamy bean-salsa spread — active 7 min, elapsed 7–9 min

In a microwave-safe bowl, combine the refried beans, Laughing Cow Light wedges, Velveeta shreds, garlic salt, garlic powder, and black pepper. Microwave about 3 minutes, then stir in the low-calorie sour cream, sriracha, and the full jar of salsa until smooth and creamy.

Step 4: Combine and drain the vegetable base — active 6 min, elapsed 6–8 min

Combine the air-fried peppers and onions with the microwaved cauliflower rice in a large container. Drain excess liquid, season lightly again if needed, and mix thoroughly.

Step 5: Warm the wraps — active 2 min, elapsed 2–3 min

Warm the egg wraps by setting them over the warm vegetable container lid, or microwave them under a damp paper towel for 15–30 seconds until flexible.

Step 6: Assemble the tacos — active 10 min, elapsed 10–15 min

Set out the vegetable base, chopped meats, bean-salsa spread, wraps, and a plate. For each taco, spread bean dip down the center, add vegetables, add meat, roll, and eat. Repeat until happily stuffed.

Cleaned recipe text

Protein

  • 200 g cooked chicken breast
  • 200 g cooked lean steak such as sirloin or flank, or whatever lean steak is on hand

Vegetable base

  • 6 bell peppers, sliced, about 900 g
  • 2 medium onions, sliced, about 300 g
  • 2 bags frozen plain cauliflower rice, about 500 g total
  • Garlic salt, garlic powder, and black pepper, to taste

Creamy bean-salsa spread

  • 1 can reduced-calorie or fat-free refried beans, about 315 g
  • 3 Laughing Cow Light wedges
  • 50 g Velveeta shredded cheese
  • Garlic salt, garlic powder, and black pepper, to taste
  • 1 tbsp low-calorie sour cream
  • Sriracha, to taste, about 1 tbsp
  • 1 jar hot salsa, about 450–500 g

Wraps and optional toppings

  • 6 Egglife or Crepini-style egg wraps
  • Optional: hot sauce, jalapeños, cilantro, lime, lettuce, or pico

Steps

  1. Start the vegetables and cauliflower rice. Slice the bell peppers and onions. Add them to the air fryer, season with garlic salt, garlic powder, and black pepper, and air fry at 390–400°F until softened, lightly charred, and slightly crisp, tossing every 5–7 minutes. Microwave the frozen cauliflower rice while the peppers and onions cook.
  2. Grill the chicken and steak. Grill the cooked-weight target amount of chicken breast first on low heat until it reaches 165°F, about 15 minutes per side depending on thickness. Let it rest, then grill the lean steak about 4 minutes per side for medium rare, or to preference. Rest briefly, then cut both meats into bite-size pieces.
  3. Make the creamy bean-salsa spread. In a microwave-safe bowl, combine the refried beans, Laughing Cow Light wedges, Velveeta shreds, garlic salt, garlic powder, and black pepper. Microwave about 3 minutes, then stir in the low-calorie sour cream, sriracha, and the full jar of salsa until smooth and creamy.
  4. Combine and drain the vegetable base. Combine the air-fried peppers and onions with the microwaved cauliflower rice in a large container. Drain excess liquid, season lightly again if needed, and mix thoroughly.
  5. Warm the wraps. Warm the egg wraps by setting them over the warm vegetable container lid, or microwave them under a damp paper towel for 15–30 seconds until flexible.
  6. Assemble the tacos. Set out the vegetable base, chopped meats, bean-salsa spread, wraps, and a plate. For each taco, spread bean dip down the center, add vegetables, add meat, roll, and eat. Repeat until happily stuffed.
