Step timing

Step 1: Start the vegetables and cauliflower rice — active 10 min, elapsed 18–25 min

Slice the bell pepper and onion. Add them to the air fryer, season with garlic salt, garlic powder, and black pepper, and air fry at 390–400°F until softened, lightly charred, and slightly crisp, tossing every 5–7 minutes. Microwave the cauliflower rice or cauliflower medley while the peppers and onions cook.

Step 2: Cook or reheat the lean protein — active 8 min, elapsed 8–15 min

Cook 93% lean ground beef until browned and cooked through, or reheat/cook your chosen lean protein. For the logged version, use 175 g 93% lean ground beef. If using chicken, cook to 165°F. If using steak, cook to preference and rest before cutting.

Step 3: Measure the bean dip spread — active 3 min, elapsed 3–5 min

Use the measured bean dip amount for the meal, logged here as 7 servings / 122 calories total. If you made the larger creamy bean-salsa batch, scoop only the amount you plan to use on the tacos and refrigerate the rest.

Step 4: Combine and drain the vegetable base — active 5 min, elapsed 5–8 min

Combine the air-fried peppers and onions with the microwaved cauliflower rice or cauliflower medley. Drain excess liquid, season lightly again if needed, and mix thoroughly.

Step 5: Warm the wraps — active 2 min, elapsed 2–3 min

Warm the Egglife wraps by setting them over the warm vegetable container lid, or microwave them under a damp paper towel for 15–30 seconds until flexible.

Step 6: Assemble the tacos — active 10 min, elapsed 10–15 min

Set out the vegetable base, cooked protein, measured bean dip, wraps, and a plate. For each taco, spread a spoonful of bean dip down the center, add vegetables, add meat, roll, and eat. Repeat for all 6 tacos.
