# Giant Egg Foo Young

A giant egg foo young / skillet quiche hybrid built for volume: vegetables, egg whites, a little whole egg, a small hit of Velveeta, optional cooked chicken, and a creamy sauce made from vegetable liquid instead of cream, butter, or flour.

## Working version

This version uses **100g cooked chicken breast** as the sweet spot: still huge, close to the original calorie target, and right around the 100g protein goal.

## Estimates

These are practical estimates based on common package sizes and typical nutrition values, not lab-tested nutrition.

- Calories: about **850**
- Protein: about **98g**
- Final plated weight: about **4.6 lb**
- Active prep time: **20–25 minutes**
- Total time: **55–65 minutes**
- Estimated plated cost: about **$14**
- Total grocery cost including leftover cooked vegetables: about **$19–21**

## Assumptions

- Frozen cauliflower: 3 x 12 oz bags
- Mushrooms: 1 x 8 oz package
- Zucchini: 1000g raw, chopped
- Air fryer: 400°F
- Green Giant vegetable + cheese packet: 110 calories
- Reduced-fat Velveeta: 10g
- Protein: 100g cooked boneless skinless chicken breast
- Egg base: 1 whole egg + 275g egg whites
- Only 1000g of cooked vegetables goes into the skillet; the rest is saved for later.

## Ingredients

### Vegetable base

- 3 bags frozen cauliflower, about 12 oz each
- 1 package mushrooms, about 8 oz, sliced or chopped
- 1000g zucchini, chopped
- Calorie-free avocado oil spray
- Garlic salt, to taste
- Garlic powder, to taste
- Onion powder, to taste
- Smoked paprika, to taste

### Sauce

- 1 Green Giant vegetable + cheese packet, 110 calories
- Liquid drained from the cooked vegetables
- 1 tablespoon sriracha, or to taste
- 2 teaspoons red miso
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic salt, or to taste
- 1–2g xanthan gum

### Skillet

- Calorie-free avocado oil spray
- 1–2 teaspoons minced garlic
- 1000g cooked vegetables from the vegetable base
- 100g cooked boneless skinless chicken breast, chopped or shredded
- 1 whole egg
- 275g egg whites
- 10g reduced-fat Velveeta, chopped or torn into small pieces

## Equipment

- Large air fryer
- Large container with lid
- Blender, ideally high-speed
- Kitchen scale
- Large nonstick skillet with lid
- Tongs
- Spatula

## Steps

### 1. Air fry the cauliflower and mushrooms

Add the frozen cauliflower and mushrooms to the air fryer basket. Spray lightly with avocado oil spray, then season with garlic salt, garlic powder, onion powder, and smoked paprika.

Start air frying at **400°F**. The vegetables will look steamy and wet at first. That is normal. The goal is to drive off moisture and build flavor.

### 2. Chop and season the zucchini

While the cauliflower and mushrooms cook, chop the zucchini. Season it separately with garlic salt, garlic powder, onion powder, and smoked paprika.

### 3. Add zucchini and finish the vegetable batch

Add the seasoned zucchini to the air fryer. Continue cooking until the vegetables are reduced, soft, lightly browned, and a little charred around some edges.

Toss several times with tongs. Total air fryer time should be about **35–40 minutes**.

### 4. Start the sauce base early

While the vegetables finish, microwave the Green Giant vegetable + cheese packet according to the package directions, about **6 minutes**.

Pour the hot packet into the blender and let it sit there while the vegetables finish. Do not blend yet.

### 5. Drain the vegetable liquid into the blender

Transfer all cooked vegetables into a large container. There should be a lot of liquid at the bottom.

Put the lid partially on the container, hold the vegetables back with the lid, tilt carefully, and pour only the liquid into the blender.

That liquid is the sauce trick: it adds flavor, volume, and body without cream, butter, or flour.

### 6. Weigh out 1000g cooked vegetables

After draining, weigh out **1000g cooked vegetables**. Only this amount goes into the skillet. Save the remaining cooked vegetables for another meal.

### 7. Blend the sauce

To the blender, add the sriracha, red miso, garlic salt, and xanthan gum.

Blend on high speed for several minutes until thick, smooth, and creamy. Scrape down the sides and lid with a spatula, then pour the sauce into a bowl.

### 8. Build the skillet and add eggs

Heat a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat, about **3/4 heat**. Spray lightly.

Add minced garlic and cook briefly until fragrant. Add the 1000g cooked vegetables and 100g cooked chicken. Mix, then spread everything evenly across the pan.

Beat together the whole egg and egg whites. Pour evenly over the vegetable-chicken mixture. Cover and cook **1 1/2–2 minutes**, until the eggs start setting and the bottom lightly browns.

### 9. Flip, melt cheese, and plate

Remove the lid. Cut the skillet mixture into about **8 sections** and flip each section quickly.

Scatter the 10g reduced-fat Velveeta over the top. Put the lid back on, turn the heat off, and let residual heat finish setting the eggs and melting the cheese.

Stack everything onto a large plate or shallow bowl. Pour the thick vegetable sauce over the top and serve hot.

## Optional variations

- Protein: chicken, steak, trout, ground turkey, or chicken-steak combo
- Vegetables: bean sprouts, cabbage, green onion, water chestnuts
- Flavor tweaks: soy sauce, ginger, white pepper, extra sriracha

## Allergens and safety notes

Contains or may contain egg, dairy, soy, mushrooms, and possibly gluten depending on packaged sauces. If using fish as the protein, it contains fish.

Use fully cooked protein. Chicken should be cooked to **165°F** before it goes into the skillet. Refrigerate leftovers promptly because this is a large high-moisture meal with eggs and protein.
