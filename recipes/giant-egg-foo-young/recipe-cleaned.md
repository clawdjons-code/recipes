# Giant Egg Foo Young

A giant egg foo young / skillet quiche hybrid built for volume: vegetables, egg whites, a little whole egg, a small hit of Velveeta, optional cooked chicken, and a creamy sauce made from vegetable liquid instead of cream, butter, or flour.

## Working version

This version uses **100g cooked chicken breast** as the sweet spot: still huge, close to the original calorie target, and right around the 100g protein goal.

## Estimates

These are practical estimates based on common package sizes and typical nutrition values, not lab-tested nutrition.

- Calories: about **850**
- Protein: about **98g**
- Final plated weight: about **4.6 lb**
- Active prep time: **20–25 minutes**
- Total time: **55–65 minutes**
- Estimated plated cost: about **$14**
- Total grocery cost including leftover cooked vegetables: about **$19–21**

## Assumptions

- Frozen cauliflower: 3 x 12 oz bags
- Mushrooms: 1 x 8 oz package
- Zucchini: 1000g raw, chopped
- Air fryer: 400°F
- Green Giant vegetable + cheese packet: 110 calories
- Reduced-fat Velveeta: 10g
- Protein: 100g cooked boneless skinless chicken breast
- Egg base: 1 whole egg + 275g egg whites
- Only 1000g of cooked vegetables goes into the skillet; the rest is saved for later.

## Ingredients

### Vegetable base

- 3 bags frozen cauliflower, about 12 oz each
- 1 package mushrooms, about 8 oz, sliced or chopped
- 1000g zucchini, chopped
- Calorie-free avocado oil spray
- Garlic salt, to taste
- Garlic powder, to taste
- Onion powder, to taste
- Smoked paprika, to taste

### Sauce

- 1 Green Giant vegetable + cheese packet, 110 calories
- Liquid drained from the cooked vegetables
- 1 tablespoon sriracha, or to taste
- 2 teaspoons red miso
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic salt, or to taste
- 1–2g xanthan gum

### Skillet

- Calorie-free avocado oil spray
- 1–2 teaspoons minced garlic
- 1000g cooked vegetables from the vegetable base
- 100g cooked boneless skinless chicken breast, chopped or shredded
- 1 whole egg
- 275g egg whites
- 10g reduced-fat Velveeta, chopped or torn into small pieces

## Equipment

- Large air fryer
- Large container with lid
- Blender, ideally high-speed
- Kitchen scale
- Large nonstick skillet with lid
- Tongs
- Spatula

## Steps

### 1. Air fry the cauliflower and mushrooms

**You’ll need:** 3 bags frozen cauliflower (about 12 oz each), 1 package mushrooms (about 8 oz, sliced or chopped), calorie-free avocado oil spray, garlic salt, garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, and a large air fryer set to **400°F**.

Add the frozen cauliflower and mushrooms to the air fryer basket. Spray lightly with avocado oil spray, then season with garlic salt, garlic powder, onion powder, and smoked paprika. Start air frying at **400°F**. The vegetables will look steamy and wet at first; the goal is to drive off moisture and build browning.

### 2. Chop and season the zucchini

**You’ll need:** 1000g zucchini, garlic salt, garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, a knife, a cutting board, and a bowl or tray.

While the cauliflower and mushrooms cook, chop **1000g zucchini**. Season it separately with garlic salt, garlic powder, onion powder, and smoked paprika so it is ready to add as soon as the first vegetables have started shrinking.

### 3. Add zucchini and finish the vegetable batch

**You’ll need:** the seasoned 1000g chopped zucchini from Step 2, the cauliflower and mushrooms already cooking in the air fryer, and tongs.

Add the seasoned zucchini to the air fryer with the cauliflower and mushrooms. Continue cooking at **400°F**, tossing several times with tongs, until everything is reduced, soft, lightly browned, and a little charred around some edges. Total air fryer time for the vegetable batch should be about **35–40 minutes**.

### 4. Start the sauce base early

**You’ll need:** 1 Green Giant vegetable + cheese packet (110 calories), a microwave, and a blender, ideally high-speed.

While the vegetables finish, microwave the **1 Green Giant vegetable + cheese packet** according to the package directions, about **6 minutes**. Pour the hot packet into the blender and let it wait there. Do not blend yet; the drained vegetable liquid from the next step finishes the sauce base.

### 5. Drain the vegetable liquid into the blender

**You’ll need:** all cooked cauliflower, mushrooms, and zucchini from the air fryer or oven; a large container with lid; the blender holding the hot Green Giant packet from Step 4; and hot water if roasting leaves little or no liquid.

Transfer all cooked vegetables to the large container. Put the lid on partially, hold the vegetables back with the lid, tilt carefully, and pour any hot vegetable liquid into the blender. Air-fried or crowded vegetables may give roughly **1/4–1/2 cup liquid**; oven-roasted vegetables may give almost none because the moisture evaporates. If you do not have enough liquid, add hot water to the blender, starting with **1/4 cup** and adding more only until the sauce blends thick and smooth. The vegetable liquid is nice when you have it, but hot water works for the oven-roasted version.

### 6. Weigh out cooked vegetables

**You’ll need:** the drained cooked vegetables from Step 5, a kitchen scale, and the selected amount of cooked vegetables for the skillet.

After draining, weigh out cooked vegetables for the skillet: **500g for the 500-calorie tier, 750g for the 700-calorie tier, or 1000g for the 900-calorie tier**. Only that measured amount goes into this serving. Save any extra cooked vegetables for another meal.

### 7. Blend the sauce

**You’ll need:** the blender with the Green Giant packet plus drained vegetable liquid or hot water, sriracha, red miso, 1/2 teaspoon garlic salt, xanthan gum, a spatula, and a bowl.

Add the sauce seasonings to the blender: **1/2 tablespoon sriracha + 1 teaspoon red miso + 1g xanthan gum for the 500-calorie tier; 3/4 tablespoon sriracha + 1 1/2 teaspoons red miso + 1.5g xanthan gum for the 700-calorie tier; or 1 tablespoon sriracha + 2 teaspoons red miso + 2g xanthan gum for the 900-calorie tier**. Add **1/2 teaspoon garlic salt**. Blend on high for several minutes until thick, smooth, and creamy. If the blender stalls or the sauce is too thick, add hot water **1 tablespoon at a time**. Scrape down the sides and lid with a spatula, then pour the sauce into a bowl so it is ready for plating.

### 8. Build the skillet and add eggs

**You’ll need:** a large nonstick skillet with lid, calorie-free avocado oil spray, minced garlic, measured cooked vegetables, cooked boneless skinless chicken breast, 1 whole egg, and egg whites.

Heat the skillet over medium-high heat, about **3/4 heat**, and spray lightly. Cook minced garlic briefly, just until fragrant: **1/2–1 teaspoon for 500 calories, about 1 teaspoon for 700 calories, or 1–2 teaspoons for 900 calories**. Add the measured cooked vegetables and cooked chicken: **500g vegetables + 50g chicken for 500 calories; 750g vegetables + 75g chicken for 700 calories; or 1000g vegetables + 100g chicken for 900 calories**. Mix, then spread everything evenly across the pan. Pour over **1 whole egg beaten with 50g egg whites for 500 calories, 150g egg whites for 700 calories, or 250g egg whites for 900 calories**. Cover and cook **1 1/2–2 minutes**, until the eggs start setting and the bottom is lightly browned.

### 9. Flip, melt cheese, and plate

**You’ll need:** the covered skillet mixture from Step 8, reduced-fat Velveeta, a spatula, the blended vegetable sauce from Step 7, and a large plate or shallow bowl.

Remove the lid. Cut the skillet mixture into about **8 sections** and flip each section quickly with a spatula. Scatter reduced-fat Velveeta over the top: **5g for 500 calories, 8g for 700 calories, or 10g for 900 calories**. Put the lid back on, turn the heat off, and let residual heat finish setting the eggs and melting the cheese. Stack everything onto a large plate or shallow bowl, pour the thick vegetable sauce over the top, and serve hot.

## Optional variations

- Protein: chicken, steak, trout, ground turkey, or chicken-steak combo
- Vegetables: bean sprouts, cabbage, green onion, water chestnuts
- Flavor tweaks: soy sauce, ginger, white pepper, extra sriracha

## Allergens and safety notes

Contains or may contain egg, dairy, soy, mushrooms, and possibly gluten depending on packaged sauces. If using fish as the protein, it contains fish.

Use fully cooked protein. Chicken should be cooked to **165°F** before it goes into the skillet. Refrigerate leftovers promptly because this is a large high-moisture meal with eggs and protein.
