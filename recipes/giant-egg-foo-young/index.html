Giant Egg Foo Young

A giant egg foo young / skillet quiche hybrid built for volume: vegetables, egg whites, cooked chicken, a little Velveeta, and a creamy sauce made from vegetable liquid instead of cream, butter, or flour.

Steps for the selected serving

1. Air fry the cauliflower and mushrooms

You’ll need:
  • 3 bags frozen cauliflower, about 12 oz each
  • 1 package mushrooms, about 8 oz, sliced or chopped
  • Calorie-free avocado oil spray
  • Garlic salt, garlic powder, onion powder, and smoked paprika
  • Large air fryer set to 400°F

Add the frozen cauliflower and mushrooms to the air fryer basket. Spray lightly with avocado oil spray, then season with garlic salt, garlic powder, onion powder, and smoked paprika. Start air frying at 400°F. The vegetables will look steamy and wet at first; the goal is to drive off moisture and build browning.

2. Chop and season the zucchini

You’ll need:
  • 1000g zucchini
  • Garlic salt, garlic powder, onion powder, and smoked paprika
  • Knife, cutting board, and bowl or tray

While the cauliflower and mushrooms cook, chop 1000g zucchini. Season it separately with garlic salt, garlic powder, onion powder, and smoked paprika so it is ready to add as soon as the first vegetables have started shrinking.

3. Finish the vegetable batch

You’ll need:
  • The seasoned 1000g chopped zucchini from Step 2
  • The cauliflower and mushrooms already cooking in the air fryer
  • Tongs

Add the seasoned zucchini to the air fryer with the cauliflower and mushrooms. Continue cooking at 400°F, tossing several times with tongs, until everything is reduced, soft, lightly browned, and a little charred around some edges. Total air fryer time for the vegetable batch should be close to 45 minutes.

4. Start the sauce base

You’ll need:
  • 1 Green Giant vegetable + cheese packet, 110 calories
  • Microwave
  • Blender, ideally high-speed

While the vegetables finish, microwave the 1 Green Giant vegetable + cheese packet according to the package directions, about 6 minutes. Pour the hot packet into the blender and let it wait there. Do not blend yet; the drained vegetable liquid from the next step finishes the sauce base.

5. Drain the vegetable liquid

You’ll need:
  • All cooked cauliflower, mushrooms, and zucchini from the air fryer or oven
  • Large container with lid
  • The blender holding the hot Green Giant packet from Step 4
  • If roasting leaves little or no liquid: hot water, 1/4 cup to start, plus more as needed

Transfer all cooked vegetables to the large container. Put the lid on partially, hold the vegetables back with the lid, tilt carefully, and pour any hot vegetable liquid into the blender. Air-fried or crowded vegetables may give roughly 1/4–1/2 cup liquid; oven-roasted vegetables may give almost none because the moisture evaporates. If you do not have enough liquid, add hot water to the blender, starting with 1/4 cup and adding more only until the sauce blends thick and smooth. The vegetable liquid is nice when you have it, but hot water works for the oven-roasted version.

6. Measure the cooked vegetables

You’ll need:
  • The drained cooked vegetables from Step 5
  • Kitchen scale
  • 1000g cooked vegetables for the selected serving

After draining, weigh out 1000g cooked vegetables for the skillet. Only that measured amount goes into this serving. Save any extra cooked vegetables for another meal.

7. Blend the sauce

You’ll need:
  • Blender with Green Giant packet plus drained vegetable liquid, or hot water if the vegetables roasted dry
  • 1 tbsp sriracha, or to taste
  • 2 tsp red miso
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic salt
  • 2g xanthan gum
  • Spatula and bowl

Add the sriracha, red miso, garlic salt, and xanthan gum to the blender. Blend on high for several minutes until the sauce is thick, smooth, and creamy. If the blender stalls or the sauce is too thick, add hot water 1 tablespoon at a time. Scrape down the sides and lid with a spatula, then pour the sauce into a bowl so it is ready for plating.

8. Build the skillet and add eggs

You’ll need:
  • Large nonstick skillet with lid, over medium-high heat / about 3/4 heat
  • Calorie-free avocado oil spray
  • 1–2 tsp minced garlic
  • 1000g cooked vegetables from Step 6
  • 100g cooked boneless skinless chicken breast, chopped or shredded
  • 1 whole egg beaten with 250g egg whites

Heat the skillet over medium-high heat, about 3/4 heat, and spray lightly. Cook the minced garlic briefly, just until fragrant. Add the measured cooked vegetables and cooked chicken, mix, then spread everything evenly across the pan. Pour the beaten whole egg and egg whites evenly over the vegetable-chicken mixture. Cover and cook 1 1/2–2 minutes, until the eggs start setting and the bottom is lightly browned.

9. Flip, melt cheese, and plate

You’ll need:
  • The covered skillet mixture from Step 8
  • 10g reduced-fat Velveeta, chopped or torn small
  • Spatula
  • The blended vegetable sauce from Step 7
  • Large plate or shallow bowl

Remove the lid. Cut the skillet mixture into about 8 sections and flip each section quickly with a spatula. Scatter the Velveeta over the top, put the lid back on, turn the heat off, and let residual heat finish setting the eggs and melting the cheese. Stack everything onto a large plate or shallow bowl, pour the thick vegetable sauce over the top, and serve hot.

Optional variations

  • Protein: chicken, steak, trout, ground turkey, or chicken-steak combo.
  • Vegetables: bean sprouts, cabbage, green onion, water chestnuts.
  • Flavor tweaks: soy sauce, ginger, white pepper, extra sriracha.

Safety

Use fully cooked protein. Chicken should be cooked to 165°F before it goes into the skillet. Refrigerate leftovers promptly.
