Ingredients for the selected serving

Vegetable base

  • 3 bags frozen cauliflower, about 12 oz each
  • 1 package mushrooms, about 8 oz, sliced or chopped
  • 1000g zucchini, chopped
  • Calorie-free avocado oil spray
  • Garlic salt, garlic powder, onion powder, and smoked paprika

Cook the full vegetable base, then weigh out 1000g cooked vegetables for the selected serving. Save any extra cooked vegetables for another meal.

Sauce

  • 1 Green Giant vegetable + cheese packet, 110 calories
  • Drained liquid from the cooked vegetables, enough to blend into a thick sauce
  • 1 tbsp sriracha, or to taste
  • 2 tsp red miso
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic salt
  • 2g xanthan gum

Skillet

  • 1–2 tsp minced garlic
  • 1000g cooked vegetables from the vegetable base
  • 100g cooked boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 1 whole egg
  • 250g egg whites
  • 10g reduced-fat Velveeta

Notes

Estimates are practical guesses based on common package sizes and typical labels. Contains or may contain egg, dairy, soy, mushrooms, and possible gluten depending on packaged sauces.

Steps for the selected serving

1. Air fry the cauliflower and mushrooms

Add frozen cauliflower and mushrooms to the air fryer, spray lightly, season, and start at 400°F. Expect steam and moisture early.

2. Chop and season the zucchini

Chop 1000g zucchini and season separately while the first vegetables cook.

3. Finish the vegetable batch

Add zucchini and cook until reduced, soft, lightly browned, and a little charred at the edges, tossing several times.

4. Start the sauce base

Microwave the 110-calorie Green Giant vegetable + cheese packet and pour it into the blender.

5. Drain the vegetable liquid

Transfer cooked vegetables to a container, use the lid to hold the vegetables back, and pour the liquid into the blender. Use enough liquid to make a thick blendable sauce.

6. Measure the cooked vegetables

Weigh out 1000g cooked vegetables for the skillet and save the rest for another meal.

7. Blend the sauce

Blend the vegetable packet, drained liquid, 1 tbsp sriracha, 2 tsp red miso, garlic salt, and 2g xanthan gum until thick and creamy.

8. Build the skillet and add eggs

Cook 1–2 tsp garlic briefly, add 1000g cooked vegetables and 100g cooked chicken, then pour over 1 whole egg plus 250g egg whites. Cover until just setting.

9. Flip, melt cheese, and plate

Cut into sections, flip, add 10g reduced-fat Velveeta, cover with heat off, then plate and sauce heavily.

Optional variations

  • Protein: chicken, steak, trout, ground turkey, or chicken-steak combo.
  • Vegetables: bean sprouts, cabbage, green onion, water chestnuts.
  • Flavor tweaks: soy sauce, ginger, white pepper, extra sriracha.

Safety

Use fully cooked protein. Chicken should be cooked to 165°F before it goes into the skillet. Refrigerate leftovers promptly.
