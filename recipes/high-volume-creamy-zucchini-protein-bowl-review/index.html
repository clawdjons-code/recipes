Prep/cook time by step

Step 1: Spiralize the zucchini12 min active / 12 min elapsed

Weigh, trim, spiralize, and bag zucchini ends.

Step 2: Air-fry the mushrooms2 min active / 5 min elapsed

Load mushrooms; shake once if practical.

Step 3: Build the uncooked zucchini base4 min active / 4 min elapsed

Combine zucchini, mushrooms, garlic, and seasonings before heat.

Step 4: Cook your chosen protein8 min active / 12 min elapsed

Timing depends on protein and method; cook fully and set aside.

Step 5: Cook zucchini and pull liquid6 min active / 12 min elapsed

Cook until softened and watery but not mush; reserve liquid.

Step 6: Blend the creamy sauce4 min active / 4 min elapsed

Blend marinara, alfredo, yogurt, miso, sriracha, powders, and reserved liquid.

Step 7: Thicken carefully with xanthan gum2 min active / 2 min elapsed

Blend in tiny pinch slowly; stop before gummy.

Step 8: Combine and gently simmer5 min active / 7 min elapsed

Add protein and sauce; keep low to avoid yogurt curdling.

Step 9: Portion and store5 min active / 6 min elapsed

Let settle, then pack as one large batch or 4–6 servings.
